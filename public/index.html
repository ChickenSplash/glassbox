How this works

  • One sampler, many viewers. A small Node.js service with no dependencies reads /proc and /sys once a second and fans the same frame out to everyone over Server-Sent Events.
  • Drawn by hand. No chart library: the graphs are plain <canvas>, scrolling smoothly between samples.
  • Locked down. The container is read-only with no Linux capabilities, and it only sees Docker through a proxy that allows two read-only calls.
  • No open ports. Traffic reaches the box through an outbound Cloudflare tunnel. No cookies, no analytics, nothing stored about you.
