About the project

Task Space combines a procedurally generated galaxy with a practical task inspector. Lists map to star systems and tasks to planets, with read-only ClickUp integration and fictional demo data.

The Rust simulation powers a Bevy client on desktop and in the browser through WebAssembly. The homelab hosts the browser build and its Axum backend; your device handles the rendering.

Holiday camp timetable builder

Premier Education Group · BMS feature

Parents couldn’t always see what their children would be doing at holiday camp. I built a timetable creator in the BMS so franchisees can create and publish schedules directly to the course booking page.

Read the case study

The problem. Timetables were created and shared separately by individual franchisees, leaving parents with little information about the day’s activities when booking.

My contribution. I built a timetable creator within the BMS, allowing franchisees to create their own schedules and publish them to the course booking page at the press of a button.

The result. Parents can see the planned activities before booking, while timetable creation and publishing sit within one workflow.
